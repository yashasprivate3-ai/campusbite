Chef's Instinct Campus

Terms of Service

1. Service Overview

CampusBite provides digital ordering and token pickup for Chef's Instinct Campus dining.

2. Ordering & Breaks

Orders can be scheduled for official campus break windows (10:30–10:45, 12:30–12:45, 14:30–14:45) at least 60 minutes in advance. Food pickup requires presenting your digital token at the counter.

3. Payments

All prices are in INR. Unpaid orders cannot be processed.
