Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how Chef’s Campus from Chef’s Instinct uses information to provide campus ordering and pickup.

Information used

We use your Google account identifier, name, email address and available profile image for sign-in; your phone number and verification status for mandatory WhatsApp verification; and your order items, pickup choices, instructions, order status, payment references and transaction status to process orders. Authentication and verification records, timestamps, and security-related network information help protect the service.

Why it is used

Information is used to authenticate users, verify phone ownership, show the relevant workspace, manage orders and stock, confirm payments, reconcile problems, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. Do not include sensitive personal information in food instructions.

Providers and access

Google provides sign-in. WhatsApp verification uses the configured messaging provider, including MSG91 and WhatsApp/Meta as applicable. Cashfree processes payments when enabled. Hosting providers, including Railway and Vercel, process information needed to operate the service. Authorised canteen staff can access order and operational information needed for their role. Payment credentials such as card details and UPI PINs are entered with the payment provider; Chef’s Campus does not need your card PIN, CVV, or UPI PIN.

Cookies and device storage

A secure session cookie maintains sign-in. Browser storage retains your cart, tracked order, and payment-attempt reference; kitchen workspaces also store batch information. These records support the application’s functions. Sign out on shared devices.

Retention and security

Account, order, payment, and security records are kept as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for records that must be retained. Access controls, server-side verification, and protected sessions reduce risk; no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information through the support contact. We may need to verify ownership and retain information needed for transactions, disputes, security, or legal obligations. Revoking Google access does not itself erase existing order records.

Updates

Changes will be published here with an updated date. Contact the canteen team to direct privacy concerns to the service operator.
