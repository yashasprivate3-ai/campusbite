Refunds & Cancellation Policy

1. Perishable Items

Because canteen meals are prepared fresh for immediate pickup, student-initiated cancellations after payment are not supported.

2. Canteen Cancellations & Refunds

If an item becomes unavailable or kitchen volume exceeds capacity, the canteen operator initiates a manual refund to the original payment source within 5–7 business days.

3. Support

Contact support@chefsinstinct.in with your token number for payment assistance.
