Contact & order support

Last updated: 22 October 2026

For help with a Chef’s Campus order, contact the team at your participating campus canteen counter during its service hours.

Email support

Chef’s Instinct: chefsinstinct@gmail.com. Include your order token or payment reference and a short description of the issue. For urgent pickup or cancellation requests, speak to the canteen team directly.

Bring the right details

Share your order token, order date, items, and a brief description of the issue. For a payment issue, provide the transaction reference and amount. If payment was deducted without an order token, show the payment confirmation to the team so it can be reconciled.

Cancellation or refund requests

Ask the canteen team as soon as possible. Requests are handled by staff; V1 does not provide an in-app cancellation button or automatic refund tracking. See the refund policy for eligibility and processing information.

Account & privacy requests

You may also ask the canteen team to direct an account correction, deletion, or privacy request to the Chef’s Campus operator. Provide only the details needed to identify your account.

Keep your credentials private

Never share your Google password, WhatsApp OTP, card PIN, CVV, or UPI PIN with canteen staff or anyone claiming to offer support. Do not post payment screenshots publicly.
