About Chef’s Campus

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Chef’s Campus is the campus food-ordering experience from Chef’s Instinct. Students can browse the available menu, choose a pickup option, and track confirmed orders at their participating campus canteen.

Built around campus pickup

Availability depends on the canteen’s published stock and service windows. Scheduled pickup must be booked at least one hour before the selected break starts. Times shown are in Indian Standard Time (IST). Preparation times are estimates.

Secure student access

Students sign in with Google and must verify their phone number through WhatsApp before entering the student workspace or ordering.

Help with an order

For food, pickup, cancellation, or payment concerns, speak to the participating canteen team and share your order token or payment reference. Read our contact information and cancellation and refund policy.
