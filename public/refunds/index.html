Cancellation & refunds

Last updated: 22 October 2026

We aim to resolve order problems fairly while avoiding unnecessary food waste. Requests are handled by the participating canteen team. V1 has no self-service cancellation or automatic refund facility.

Before preparation starts

Request cancellation immediately at the canteen counter with your order token. If the team confirms that preparation has not started and can stop the order, the cancellation is eligible for a full refund. A request alone does not cancel the order; wait for staff confirmation.

After preparation starts

Change-of-mind cancellation is normally unavailable once preparation has started, or the food is ready or collected. Missing your pickup does not normally qualify for a refund. This does not affect remedies for a service failure or your rights under applicable law.

When something goes wrong

If the canteen cannot fulfil a paid order, you are eligible for a full refund for the unfulfilled order. For a missing, incorrect, or unacceptable item, tell the team promptly, ideally at pickup. The team will check the issue and offer an appropriate replacement or a refund for the affected item; a full refund is appropriate if the whole order cannot be used. A replacement is subject to availability and your agreement.

Deducted payment, duplicate charge, or no order

If money was deducted without a confirmed order, contact the team with the payment reference, date, and amount. Staff must reconcile the payment with the provider before confirming a refund. A confirmed duplicate successful charge is eligible for refund of the duplicate amount. Pending or failed payments may reverse through your bank or payment provider; please check the payment status before retrying.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are initiated manually by the operator through the payment provider, to the original payment method. We do not substitute app credit or require another purchase. Ask the team for confirmation and a refund reference once initiated.

For Cashfree payments, the provider generally advises 5–7 business days for the credit after initiation, depending on your bank and payment method. This is an estimate, not an instant-refund promise. If the credit is delayed, contact the team with the refund reference for follow-up. See Cashfree’s customer guide.

Request help

Use the contact and support instructions. Share your order token or transaction reference privately; never send your OTP, CVV, or UPI PIN.
