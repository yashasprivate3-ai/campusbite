Chef’s Campus · Student information

Terms of service

Last updated: 22 October 2026

These terms apply to the Chef’s Campus ordering service from Chef’s Instinct at participating campus canteens. By using the service, you agree to these terms and our privacy policy.

Accounts and access

Use accurate account and phone details. Student access requires Google sign-in and WhatsApp phone verification. Keep your credentials private. Staff tools are restricted to authorised roles; you must not attempt to access another person’s account or orders.

Menu, price and availability

Check the selected items, quantities, total, pickup method, and pickup time before paying. Prices are displayed in Indian rupees. The checkout total is the amount payable. Menu availability is subject to stock; items in your cart are not a guarantee of availability.

Order confirmation and payment

An order is confirmed only after the server verifies successful payment and displays an order token. A debit notification or checkout return alone is not confirmation. If a payment is pending, check its status before paying again. Online checkout is available only when enabled for the service.

Pickup and food information

Collect your food at the participating canteen using the order token. Scheduled pickup must be booked at least one hour before the selected break starts; all service times use IST. Preparation and readiness times are estimates. Ask the canteen about ingredients and allergens before ordering; do not assume an item is free from allergens or cross-contact.

Cancellations and service problems

Cancellation and refund requests are staff-assisted under our cancellation and refund policy. Report incorrect, missing, or unsafe food promptly. Do not consume food you believe is unsafe.

Fair use and your rights

Do not misuse the service, submit fraudulent requests, interfere with ordering, or share staff access. Access may be restricted to protect accounts and operations. These terms do not exclude rights or remedies available under applicable law. Policy updates will be published on this page; the policy in effect when an order was placed applies to that order.
